
# XStore Sales Dashboard & Sales Forecast

## 📊 Project Overview

The **XStore Sales Dashboard & Forecast** is an end-to-end **Power BI data analytics project** designed to analyze historical sales performance and provide a **forward-looking sales forecast**. The dashboard helps stakeholders quickly understand **sales trends, profitability, customer segments, geographic performance, and future expectations** using interactive and visually rich reports.

This project demonstrates practical skills in **data cleaning, modeling, DAX, visualization design, and time-series forecasting**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Contribute to the **success of a business** by leveraging data analysis techniques
* Focus specifically on **time-series analysis** to understand historical sales behavior
* Generate **valuable, actionable insights** for decision-makers
* Provide **accurate sales forecasting** to support planning, budgeting, and inventory management

## 📌 Key Insights

* Strong sales growth observed in **Q4**
* **California** is the highest revenue-generating state
* **Consumer segment** drives nearly half of total sales
* **Standard shipping** dominates order fulfillment
* Technology and Office Supplies lead category sales
